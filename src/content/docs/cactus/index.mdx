## What is CactusMod?

CactusMod is a **client-side mod** for Minecraft: Java Edition, built for the Fabric loader and recent NeoForge versions, and your go-to toolkit packed with powerful tools, quality of life enhancements, and highly customizable features for all types of players.

Instead of bundling everything, Cactus organizes its features into **content packs** you can toggle on and off as you like, so your game never gets bloated with things you don't use.
The features that exist are kept in a simple, well-known Minecraft style: most things should explain themselves, and almost everything is customizable - from the accent color and chat prefix to module behavior.
Cactus is actively maintained and developed together with its community; feature requests are taken into consideration and bugs get fixed.
